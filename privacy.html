Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

At KRM Brite, we respect your privacy. When you place an order or interact with our site, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal details such as your Name, Email address, Phone number, and Shipping Address.
  • Authentication data if you sign in using Google Auth.
  • Order history and interaction logs on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:

  • To process and fulfill your orders effectively.
  • To communicate with you regarding your order status and updates.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your queries.
  • To improve our website's functionality and user experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with our business partners. We share necessary shipping details with our logistics partner (ST Courier) strictly for the delivery of your orders.

4. Data Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information and transaction data. Our database is securely managed using Firebase Firestore.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, correction of any inaccurate information, or deletion of your account and personal data from our systems. You can reach out to our support team to exercise these rights.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at krmbrite1@gmail.com.
