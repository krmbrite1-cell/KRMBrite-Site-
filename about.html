About KRM Brite

Our Story

At KRM Brite, we recognized a simple truth in the cleaning industry: consumers are often paying mostly for water. Traditional floor cleaners and detergents come in bulky plastic bottles filled with up to 90% water. We decided to change that narrative.

Our journey began with the vision to create a product that delivers uncompromising quality, powerful cleaning, and outstanding value. We engineered a highly concentrated formula where one 500ml bottle is capable of making 5 full litres of premium cleaner right in your own home. You just add the water.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide households with safe, eco-friendly, and powerful cleaning solutions. We aim to reduce plastic waste and carbon footprint by shipping pure concentrate, all while ensuring that your home environment remains hygienic and fresh.

Why Choose Us?

  • Unmatched Value: Stop paying for water. Our 500ml concentrate gives you 5 litres of powerful cleaner, offering the best value for your money.
  • Hospital-Grade Protection: Engineered to kill 99.9% of viruses and bacteria, leaving your floors truly hygienic and safe for children and pets.
  • Eco-Friendly Formula: Made with natural-based ingredients that are tough on dirt but gentle on the planet.
  • Long-Lasting Freshness: Infused with a premium French Paradise scent that keeps your home smelling amazing for 24 hours.
