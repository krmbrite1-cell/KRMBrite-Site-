Our Story

At KRM Brite, we recognized a simple truth in the cleaning industry: consumers are often paying mostly for water. Traditional floor cleaners and detergents come in bulky plastic bottles filled with up to 90% water. We decided to change that narrative.

Our journey began with the vision to deliver uncompromising quality, powerful cleaning, and outstanding value. We engineered a highly concentrated formula where one bottle makes 5 full litres of premium cleaner right in your own home. You just add the water.

Our Mission

To provide households with safe, eco-friendly, and powerful cleaning solutions. We aim to reduce plastic waste and transport footprint by delivering pure, active concentrate, while keeping your living spaces hygienic, germ-free, and brilliantly fresh.
